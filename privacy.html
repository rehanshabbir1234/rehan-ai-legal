Privacy Policy for Rehan AI

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Rehan AI is an AI-powered chatbot developed by Rehan Digital Solutions. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you interact with our chatbot or contact us through supported services.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, we may process messages you send, your replies from the chatbot, conversation identifiers, and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to generate chatbot responses, provide customer support, maintain service functionality, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service against misuse.

3. WhatsApp

If you contact Rehan AI through WhatsApp, your messages may be processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform and our chatbot system to generate replies. WhatsApp and Meta may process information according to their own policies and terms.

4. Website Chat

If website chat features are available, information submitted through those features may be processed to respond to your questions. The exact information stored depends on the features and configuration in use.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the chatbot, including messaging and hosting providers. We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

6. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described above and subject to the system's actual configuration and applicable requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may stop messaging the chatbot at any time. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and any necessary verification.

8. Children's Privacy

Rehan AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
