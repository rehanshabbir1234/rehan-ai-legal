Data Deletion Instructions for Rehan AI

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Rehan AI is an AI-powered chatbot developed by Rehan Digital Solutions. If you have interacted with Rehan AI and want to request deletion of personal information associated with your conversations, you can contact us using the details below.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Email support@rehandigitalsolutions.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Rehan AI Data Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us which service you used, such as WhatsApp or website chat, and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant records.
  4. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary sensitive information.

What Happens Next?

We will review your request and may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and locate the relevant data. We will explain the outcome and delete eligible information where required by applicable law and our actual data-retention obligations.

Some information may need to be retained where legally required or necessary to resolve security, fraud-prevention, or legal matters. Copies held independently by WhatsApp, Meta, or other providers may need to be managed through their own privacy and deletion processes.
